Bitwaddle

Bitwaddle is a Vietnamese educational channel that explains programming concepts to beginners with short animated videos and everyday metaphors.

Bitwaddle Studio

Bitwaddle Studio is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Bitwaddle channel to manage and publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own social media accounts, including TikTok. It is not offered to other users and does not publish on behalf of anyone else.
